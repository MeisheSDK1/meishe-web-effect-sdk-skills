Service objects
-----------------------
-----------------------
The Effect SDK of Meisei mainly covers the functions of special effects rendering. Its application scenarios only use Meisei's SDK to render special effects such as filters, subtitles, stickers, props, etc. It is often applied to scenes such as shooting special effects processing, live broadcasting, VR, etc. Developers can independently use all the special effects capabilities of the SDK according to their own needs. Meishu company will at any time according to the changes of the web environment, use scenarios, quickly adjust, improve, upgrade the SDK toolkit, to ensure the stability of the SDK package, high efficiency, high compatibility, to bring good service experience to developers.

Capabilities and limitations
-----------------------
-----------------------

* Extension pack format (extension pack is a content package used by Meishe to carry extended materials, including captions, filters, stickers, facemeshs, makeups, etc.):
	* Captions: .captionstyle
	* Filters: .videofx
	* Stickers: .animatedsticker
	* Transitions: .videotransition
	* Props: .arscene
	* Facemeshs: .facemesh
	* Makeups: .makeup

### Background segmentation
Meishe Effect SDK is based on Meishe's self-developed machine learning to identify human body contours in images and videos and separate them from the background. Combined with Meishe SDK's rendering engine, it provides corporate users with rich visual effects.
